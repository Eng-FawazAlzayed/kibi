KIBI privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

KIBI is a personal app used only by its owner. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What KIBI accesses

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): to add events the owner asks for and to read the owner's schedule back to them.
  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): to search the owner's inbox when asked and to summarize new mail. KIBI never sends, deletes or changes mail.

Where the data goes

  • The phone connects directly to Google. KIBI's server doesn't hold the owner's Google sign-in. By default, new mail notifications and answers about mail are built on the phone with simple rules, and KIBI doesn't send the emails to an AI model.
  • If the owner turns on "smarter summaries", the text of new mail worth a notification, and the details and preview text of mail matching a question in chat, are sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) through the owner's own server. Mail recognized as a one-time code is never sent this way. Promotions aren't sent for notifications, but can be sent if they match a question.
  • KIBI's replies about mail, which can include senders and subjects, are saved in the owner's chat history. That history is end-to-end encrypted on the owner's server and shared with their other devices. Recent history can be sent to Claude with later chat messages, even with "smarter summaries" off, unless the owner uses on-device mode.
  • What the owner says when adding a calendar event may also be sent to Claude through that server, so KIBI can understand the request.
  • The server is designed to pass this text to Claude without storing it. Anthropic handles it under its own terms.
  • KIBI doesn't sell Google data or use it for advertising, and doesn't transfer it to anyone except as described on this page.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect in KIBI's Settings, or remove KIBI at myaccount.google.com/connections.
